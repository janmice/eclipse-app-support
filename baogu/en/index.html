Support

Can I fill in missing details after taking a photo?

When adding a device, photograph a model label, receipt or warranty card, or select an image from Photos. Review the suggested values and type anything missing. You can edit the record again after saving.

How do I track different warranties for one device?

Edit the device and add each part under Additional warranties. Give each its own start date, term and reminder. Use custom fields for a retailer, extended warranty ID or other details.

Why didn’t I receive a reminder?

Check the expiry date and reminder for that warranty. The scheduled time must still be in the future. Enable notifications for Baogu in iPhone Settings. Reminders are scheduled for 9 a.m. Taiwan time.

How do I share a receipt or move to a new phone?

Choose the details and original photos on the device page, preview them and share. To move your records, export a backup in Settings and merge it on your new phone. Existing devices are skipped.

How do I restore Pro?

Sign in with the Apple Account you used to purchase Pro. Open the one-time purchase in Settings and select Restore purchases. Import your backup separately to recover devices and photos.

How do I track returns, repairs and maintenance?

Add free return and registration tasks on the device page. You can attach confirmation and keep part numbers and packaging photos on supply cards. Pro tracks repair cases and pickups, parts warranties, household maintenance and shopping.

Contact Baogu support

Use the developer’s GitHub support page and include the app version and the steps that led to the issue. Posts are public. Redact serial numbers, contact details and receipts.
