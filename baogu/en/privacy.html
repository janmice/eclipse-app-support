Privacy Policy

Published by CHENYANG Chou · Effective November 23, 2026

Device records and text recognition

Device names, models, serial numbers, warranties, custom fields, repairs and selected photos are stored on your device. Text recognition runs on the device. The app requires no account and includes no ads, analytics or developer-operated automatic sync. Purchase tasks, service cases, contact details, supplies and maintenance history are also stored on your device.

Camera, photos, notifications and purchases

The app uses the camera when you take a photo. The system photo picker provides only the photos you select. Your iPhone delivers reminders, which you can turn off in Settings. Apple handles Pro purchases and restores. The app uses verified transaction status to enable Pro and does not request payment card details.

Sharing, backups and deletion

You choose the fields, original photos and destination when sharing. Exported backup files include device records and photos and are not encrypted by the app. You can delete a device and its attachments in the app. Copies saved or shared elsewhere must be managed separately. iCloud Drive and device backups are subject to the policies of the service you choose.

Website and support

This site uses GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information to operate and secure the site. GitHub processes comments you choose to post publicly, and the developer uses them to answer your request. GitHub Privacy Statement
